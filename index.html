The best thing I have read lately

The best thing I've read lately has to be an analysis I read on my favourite football team, Arsenal. It went into detail about how the coach,
Mikel Arteta's attacking system heavily relies on the fullbacks. It also talked how the club's new signing, Bruno Guimaraes could be the much
needed balance the team has to been looking for in order to break down low blocks in games. It really captured my attention because I'm someone
who not only loves Arsenal but also loves the way they play and to get an almost flawless breakdown of the ideology that's behind the coach's tactics
was very insightful. I already had my own thoughts and assumptions about them and I did actually end up getting some of them right but to not only
confirm but also learn more things to take onboard was really amazing.
